# Reporting Security Issues

The Mayura AI team and community take security bugs in Mayura AI seriously. We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings, and will make every effort to acknowledge your contributions.

To report a security issue, open a private advisory on the affected repository: go to its **Security** tab, then **Report a vulnerability**. Reporting on the repository the bug lives in means the right maintainers see it first. If you are unsure which repository is affected, or the issue is not tied to one, use the [organization advisory form](https://github.com/mayurahq/.github/security/advisories/new).

Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. If you can't use GitHub advisories, email support@mayura.ai instead.

The Mayura AI team will send a response indicating the next steps in handling your report. After the initial reply to your report, the security team will keep you informed of the progress towards a fix and full announcement, and may ask for additional information or guidance.

Report security bugs in third-party dependencies to the person or team maintaining that dependency, through its own ecosystem's reporting channel.

Individual repositories may publish their own `SECURITY.md` with a narrower trust boundary or a different contact. Where one exists, it takes precedence over this policy.
